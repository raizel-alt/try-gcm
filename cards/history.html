How We Began.

1984 — Golden Century Marketing officially started its business operations. Our very first warehouse and office were located at Divisoria, running with a humble but dedicated team of just 5 Salesmen and 1 Staff.

Initial Principals:

  • Consolidated Distillers of Far East (Club Valentino Rum)
  • Lamoiyan Corporation (Hapee Toothpaste)
  • Permex Producer & Exporter Corp. (Paradiso Sardines & Tuna)
  • LG Goods Corporation (Biscuits)
  • MV Foods Corporation (Richie Juices)

Initial Coverage:
Camiguin, Gingoog City, Misamis Oriental, Bukidnon, Cagayan de Oro, Lanao del Norte, and Iligan City.
